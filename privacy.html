Kitchen Notebook privacy

This personal household app stores recipes, photos, meal plans, groceries and pantry records in your selected Google Drive notebook. An offline copy and pending changes are stored in your browser on each device.

The app requests permission for files it creates or you select for it. It does not request unrestricted Drive access. Google sign-in takes place on Google’s screens. Access tokens are kept in memory; the app does not store your Google password or client secret.

When an optional public recipe reader is configured, recipe-link addresses are sent to that Google Apps Script deployment. Photo and PDF recognition takes place on your device. This app does not send your household records to ChatGPT, Render or the public website repository.

There are no app analytics or advertising scripts. Google provides its own authorization and file-picker services. Disconnecting clears this device’s household copy after pending changes have synced; your Google Drive notebook remains available. You can export your household records from the Household screen.
